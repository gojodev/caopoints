Overview

Why use this tool?

When I was an Irish Leaving Cert student it was frustrating and time-consuming to calculate an ideal grade requirement to reach a CAO points target.

So I built this tool.

It quickly outputs the lowest grades you need across your subjects to hit a specific points total — no trial and error required.

For example, finding easy ways to score 400 CAO points with 4 HL and 2 OL subjects involves a lot of combinations. This handles that instantly.

The tool is completely free to use and never stores any of your information.

TLDR — saves students time, stress, and money.
